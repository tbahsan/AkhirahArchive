## 1. 3-Tier Evidentiary Framework

| Tier | Meaning | Reading & Verification Posture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Primary Revelation | Noble Qur'an (`quran.com`) and authentic *Sahih*/*Hasan* Sunnah (`sunnah.com`, `dorar.net`). Sole binding foundation of creed. |
| **Tier 2** | Scholarly Research & Classical Commentaries | Verified academic works, classical hadith commentaries (*Fath al-Bari*, *Sharh Sahih Muslim*, *Sharh al-Tahawiyyah*), and mainstream hadith-grounded lecture series. Works with mixed historical chains carry **Isnad Caution**. |
| **Tier 3** | Contemporary / Speculative Discourse | Modern geopolitical essays, youth talks blending classical narrations with personal technological/political projections, or speculative forecasts. Always verify individual hadiths in Tier-1/Tier-2 sources; exact dates belong to Allah alone (Qur'an 7:187; 31:34). |
